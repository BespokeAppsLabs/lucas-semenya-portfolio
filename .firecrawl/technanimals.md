### NSFAS Dashboard01

SA based - Student Accommodation Management System

The NSFAS Student Accommodation Management System is designed to improve the experience of students living in accommodations funded by the National Student Financial Aid Scheme. It integrates various features such as real-time availability tracking, automated allocation processes, and comprehensive reporting tools to efficiently manage student accommodations. This system aims to meet the unique requirements of educational institutions while optimizing accommodation resources.
